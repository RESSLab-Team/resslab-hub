Round 2 — structural alphabet wordmarks

A custom single-stroke caps alphabet (the H is a portal frame). Not pursued — caps-only lost the camel-case name.

Round 3 — flat structural marks

Frames on base plates. S1 (braced bay) was the first keeper.

Round 4 — chunky + 3D braced bays

Ground bar added; corner views. A1 was briefly shipped.

Round 5 — test-data marks

The database's content as a mark. Distinctive, judged too abstract for the app's face.

Round 6 — technical-drawing logos

Double-line members, overshooting joints, hairline stays, bolts, footings. The winning family.
